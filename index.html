05CTB · 5600K

Start here.

Trained to build it.
Learning why it sells.

Hi, I'm Samuel. I studied Infocomm & Media Engineering at Nanyang Polytechnic — lighting rigs, sensors, the software that keeps them talking to each other. Most of what I built was made to be walked up to by a stranger, which is a harder brief than it sounds.

That is what pulled me towards the business side. Once you have spent a week making a corridor more inviting than the one next to it, you stop seeing marketing as decoration and start seeing it as the same problem with different tools. I want to understand the decision, not just the installation.

Photography is the hobby that keeps feeding it — it taught me to watch people rather than scenery. Off the clock I cook without measuring and lose at games.
