A creative at heart,
an engineer by training.

Hi, I’m Samuel. I studied Infocomm & Media Engineering (DIME) at Nanyang Polytechnic, where I developed a strong technical foundation and a curiosity for understanding how technology works and how it can be used to create meaningful experiences.

Beyond engineering, I’m a creative at heart with a deep love for photography. I enjoy capturing moments that pass too quickly and turning them into something that lasts. Photography has shaped the way I see the world — teaching me to pay attention to people, emotions, and the details that often go unnoticed.

Today, I’m looking to build beyond my technical foundation and explore the world of business, particularly the psychology behind consumer behaviour. I’m fascinated by what influences people’s decisions, what makes them connect with a brand, and how creativity and technology can come together to meet those needs.
